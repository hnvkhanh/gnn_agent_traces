Reading these numbers

  • When the LLM does not attack by itself, a synthetic attacker step is appended at the end of the trace, so attack traces always end with a reject. The real-only scores in results/REPORT.md leave those steps out.
  • Numbers are means over 3 seeds. With a few hundred traces per benchmark, differences under about 0.05 are within noise.
